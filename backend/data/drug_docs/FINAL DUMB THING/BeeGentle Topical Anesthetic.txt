description:
  BeeGentle™ is a water-insoluble topical anesthetic containing 20% benzocaine which forms an insoluble film upon contact with moist tissue. The film releases benzocaine at a constant rate over a 20-30 minute period. The anesthetic adheres to soft tissue forming a white film that remains in place until physically removed or until slowly dissolved by saliva. This unique time-release benzocaine anesthetic provides longer lasting anesthesia versus conventional water-soluble products. BeeGentle has an ethanol based solvent.
