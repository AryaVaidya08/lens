  Warnings For rectal use only. This package is intended for in-institution use only. Alcohol Warning: If you consume 3 or more alcoholic drinks every day, ask your doctor whether you should take acetaminophen or other pain relievers/fever reducers. Acetaminophen may cause liver damage. Do not use in children under 6 years. Stop use and ask a doctor if fever lasts more than 3 days (72 hours), or recurs or if you need to use this product for pain for more than 10 days continuously (adults) or 5 days (children). Severe or recurrent pain, or high or continued fever may indicate a serious illness. If pregnant or breast-feeding, ask a health professional before use. If swallowed or in case of overdose, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away. Quick medical attention is critical in case of overdose for adults and for children even if you do not notice any signs or symptoms.
