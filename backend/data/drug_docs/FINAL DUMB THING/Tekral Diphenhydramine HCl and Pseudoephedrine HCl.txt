stop_use:
  Stop use and ask a doctor If nervousness, dizziness, or sleeplessness occur. A persistent cough may be a sign of a serious condition. If cough or other symptoms do not improve within 7 days, tend to recur, or are accompanied by fever, rash, or persistent headache, consult a doctor.
do_not_use:
  Do not use with any other product containing diphenhydramine, even one used on skin If you have a breathing problem such as emphysema or chronic bronchitis If you are now taking a prescription monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI) (certain drugs for depression, psychiatric, or emotional conditions, or Parkinson's disease), or for 2 weeks after stopping the MAOI drug. If you do not know if your prescription drug contains an MAOI, ask a doctor or pharmacist before taking this product
