  Warnings Do not use if you are now taking a prescription monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI) (certain drugs for depression, psychiatric or emotional conditions, or Parkinson’s disease), or for 2 weeks after stopping the MAOI drug. If you do not know if your prescription drug contains an MAOI, ask a doctor or pharmacist before taking this product. Ask a doctor before use if you have persistent cough or chronic cough such as occurs with smoking, asthma, chronic bronchitis, emphysema cough accompanied by excessive phlegm (mucus) Stop use and ask a doctor if cough lasts more than 7 days or occurs with fever, rash, or headaches that lasts.
