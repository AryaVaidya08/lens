  Warnings Liver warning This product contains acetaminophen. The maximum daily dose of this product is 6 tablespoonfuls in 24 hours. Severe liver damage may occur if:you take more than 8 tablespoonfuls (4,000mg of acetaminophen) in 24 hours with other drugs containing acetaminophen 3 or more alcoholic drinks everyday while using the product. Allergy alert: Acetaminophen may cause severe skin reactions. Symptoms may include: skin reddening blisters rash If a skin reaction occurs, stop use and seek medical help right away.
