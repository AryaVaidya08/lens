Do not take this product if you have difficulty in swallowing. If you experience chest pain, vomiting, or difficulty in swallowing or breathing after taking this product, seek immediate medical attention. Ask a doctor before use if you have abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting or rectal bleeding a sudden change in bowel habits that persist over a period of 2 weeks constipation associated with gastrointestinal disorders Ask a pharmacist before use if you are taking any other drug.
