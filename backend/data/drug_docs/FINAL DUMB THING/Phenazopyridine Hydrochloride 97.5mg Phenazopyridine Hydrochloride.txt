Caution: Do not use this product if you have glucose-6-phosphate Dehydrogenase (G6PD) deficiency unless approved by your physician. Stop use and ask doctor if • your symptoms last for more than 2 days • you suspect you are having an adverse reaction to the medication. If pregnant or breastfeeding , ask health professional before use.
