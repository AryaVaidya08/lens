  Ask a doctor or pharmacist before use if you are taking minocycline, doxycycline, tetracycline or are on coumadin therapy; zinc treatment may inhibit the absorption of these medicines.

  When using this product to avoid minor stomach upset, do not dissolve lozenges on an empty stomach avoid citrus fruits or juices and products containing citric acid 1/2 hour before or after taking, as they may diminish effectiveness.

  Stop use and ask doctor if symptoms persist for more than 7 days or are accompanied by a fever.

  Directions adults and children 12 years and over for best results, begin treatment at start of symptoms (within 24 - 48 hours of onset) completely dissolve lozenges in mouth. (Do not swallow whole.
