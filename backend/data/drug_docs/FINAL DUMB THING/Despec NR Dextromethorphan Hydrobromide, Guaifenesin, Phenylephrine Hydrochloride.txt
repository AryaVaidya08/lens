If you do not know if your prescription drug contains an MAOI, ask a doctor or pharmacist before taking this product. dosage_and_administration:
  Directions Do not exceed recommended dosage. Drink plenty of water. Adults and children 12 years of age and over: 1 teaspoonful (5mL) every 4 hours. Not to exceed 6 teaspoonfuls in a 24 hour period. Children 6-12 years of age: 1/2 teaspoonful (2.5 mL) every 4 hours. Not to exceed 3 teaspoonfuls in a 24 hour period. Children 2-6 years of age: 1 dropperful every 4 hours. Not to exceed 6 dropperfuls in a 24 hour period. Children under 2 years of age: Consult a physician.

  If pregnant or breast-feeding, ask a health professional before use.

  Stop use and ask a doctor if cough or nasal congestion persists for more than 1 week, tends to recur, or is accompanied by a fever, rash or persistent headache. These could be signs of a serious condition. nervousness, dizziness, or sleeplessness occur new symptoms occur
