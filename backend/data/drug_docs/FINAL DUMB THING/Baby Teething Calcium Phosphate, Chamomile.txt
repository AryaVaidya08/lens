  Directions Children under 3 years of age Dissolve 2 to 3 tablets on tongue 4 times daily. If you prefer, tablets may be dissolved in a teaspoon of water and then given to the child. If the child is restless or wakeful, dissolve 2 tablets on tongue every hour for up to 6 doses. Teething Tablets are very soft and dissolve almost instantly in the mouth. Please note: if your child has been crying or upset, your child may fall asleep after using this product because the pain has been relieved and your child can rest.
