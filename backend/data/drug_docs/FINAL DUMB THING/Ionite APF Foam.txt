  Remove cap from can. Prior to each use, shake can thoroughly for at least 15 seconds. To dispense,invert the can completely upside down. Instruct patient to bite down and leave the tray in contact with the teeth between 1 - 4 minutes. Use a saliva ejector during treatment to minimize ingestion of product.
