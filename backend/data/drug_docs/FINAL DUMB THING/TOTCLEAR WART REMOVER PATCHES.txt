  Uses: For the removal of common warts. For the removal of plantar warts on the bottom of the foot. The common wart is easily recognized by the rough "cauliflower-like" appearance of the surface. The plantar wart is recognized by its location only on the bottom of the foot, it's tenderness, and the intteruption of the footprint pattern.
