pregnancy:
  Pregnancy: Category C Animal reproduction studies have not been conducted with urea cream or lotion. It is also not known whether urea cream or lotion can cause fetal harm when administered to a pregnant woman or can affect reproductive capacity. Urea cream or lotion should be given to a pregnant woman only if clearly needed.
