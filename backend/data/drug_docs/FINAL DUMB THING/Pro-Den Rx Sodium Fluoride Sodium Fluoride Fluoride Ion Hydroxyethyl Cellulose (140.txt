dosage_and_administration:
  DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION Apply daily after usual brushing and flossing at bedtime or more often if your dentist recommends additional therapy based on your diagnosis. Cover brush head with Pro-DenRx 1.1% Neutral Sodium Fluoride Gel and brush around all tooth surfaces and gum line for at least one minute. Spit out gel. Adults: Wait 30 minutes before rinsing mouth; Children under 12: Immediately after use, rinse mouth thoroughly with water. When using a mouthpiece or applicator, cover the inner surface with gel.
