  Directions • clean the affected area and dry thoroughly • apply a thin layer of the product over affected area twice daily (morning and night) or as directed by a doctor • supervise children in the use of this product For athlete's foot: • use daily for 4 weeks. If condition persists longer, consult a doctor. • pay special attention to the spaces between the toes • wear well-fitting, ventilated shoes • change shoes and socks at least once daily For ringworm, use daily for 4 weeks.
