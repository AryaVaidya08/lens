Administering enema: • With steady pressure, gently insert enema tip into rectum with a slight side-to-side movement, with tip pointing toward navel. It is not necessary to empty the bottle completely, as it contains more liquid than needed. • Remove enema tip from rectum. • Retain enema in accordance with doctor's instructions.
