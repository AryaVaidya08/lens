The chance is higher if you: Are 60 years or older, have had stomach ulcers or bleeding problems, take a blood thinning (anticoaglant) or steroid drug, take other drugs containing a NSAID [aspirin, ibuprofen, naproxen, or others], have 3 or more alcoholic drinks every day while using this product, or take more / for a longer time than directed.
