spl_unclassified_section:
  For Topical Dermatological and External Use Only Rx Only
  INSTRUCTIONS FOR USE Important: Prime Can Before Initial Use. To Prime Can: Shake can well (until product moves inside can). Firmly strike bottom of can onto palm of hand at least 3 times. Hold can upright and direct initial spray to a non-skin surface. Until primed, DO NOT spray directly on the skin as the initial spray may expel cold liquid propellant. Press down on actuator for 1-3 seconds until foam begins to dispense. If foam does not dispense within 3 seconds, prime can again. During Use: Holding can upright, dispense Hylatopic ® Emollient Foam into palm of hand and apply to affected area 3 times per day, or as directed by a physician. Massage gently into the skin until completely absorbed. If the skin is broken, cover with appropriate dressing. Wipe off any excess foam from actuator after use.
