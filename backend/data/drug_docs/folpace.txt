adverse_reactions:
  ADVERSE REACTIONS Allergic sensitization has been reported following both oral and parenteral administration of folic acid. Paresthesia and somnolence have been reported with pyridoxine. Mild transient diarrhea, polycythemia vera, itching transitory exanthema and feeling of swelling of entire body have been associated with cobalamins. There are occasional reports of hypersensitivity reactions (rash, pruritis, urticaria) in those receiving parenteral hydroxocobalamin and possibly oral hydroxocobalamin.
